Concept demos, not client data

These examples use sample data to show the idea. They are not case studies and they do not claim real client results.

  • Customisable to your needs. The screens, queues and reminders are built around how your business actually runs — not a fixed template.
  • Approvals stay with your team. The console highlights what needs attention and prepares the next step; nothing customer-facing is sent without review.
  • Start small, then build. Begin with one simple automated process, see it working, and add more only when it earns its place.
