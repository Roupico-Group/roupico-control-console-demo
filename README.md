# Roupiço Business Control Console demos

Interactive concept demos showing how a tailored control console could bring business tasks, approvals and follow-up workflows into one clearer place.

## Demo sectors

- Trades and service businesses — includes a guided two-minute walkthrough
- Salons and beauty businesses
- Health and safety administration
- Portuguese property mediation

All names, records, figures and interactions are simulated. These pages are product concepts, not client projects or claims of real-world results. Customer-facing and sensitive actions remain explicitly subject to human review.
